A Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) is an advanced practice registered nurse who specializes in mental health. PMHNPs are trained to evaluate, diagnose, and treat psychiatric conditions — including prescribing and managing medications. Alex is board-certified by the ANCC and holds full practice authority in California.
The initial evaluation is 60 minutes. Follow-up appointments are typically 15–30 minutes depending on your needs and the complexity of your care.
Yes, when clinically appropriate. As a Board-Certified PMHNP, Alex is fully licensed to prescribe and manage psychiatric medications. Medication is always discussed collaboratively — never prescribed without your understanding and consent.

Mindful Methods Advanced Nursing Practice, Inc. provides telehealth psychiatric services to adults 18 and older throughout California. Alex Renteria, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, PHN is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner licensed in California. Services provided are psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and follow-up care — not psychotherapy or crisis services.
