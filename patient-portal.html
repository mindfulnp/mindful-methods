How It Works

  1. 1 Log in with the email you used at your first appointment.
  2. 2 Request or confirm upcoming appointments with your provider.
  3. 3 Complete or update intake and consent forms securely.
  4. 4 Access your documents and messages through your secure account.

Telehealth Visits

All appointments are conducted via secure telehealth — no app download needed, just a device with a camera and internet connection.

Once your appointment is confirmed, you'll receive an email with a link to join your visit.

Mindful Methods Advanced Nursing Practice, Inc. provides telehealth psychiatric services to adults 18 and older throughout California. Alex Renteria, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, PHN is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner licensed in California. Services provided are psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and follow-up care — not psychotherapy or crisis services.

If you are experiencing a psychiatric emergency, please call 911, go to your nearest emergency room, or call the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by dialing or texting 988. This website is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice or establish a patient-provider relationship.
